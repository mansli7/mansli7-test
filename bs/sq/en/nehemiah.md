Nehemiah 1–4

---


1. What did Nehemiah hear that caused him to sit down, weep, mourn, fast, and pray before the God of heaven, and in his prayer, from which book did he ask the LORD to remember the words He had commanded Moses?

2. Whose gracious hand was on Nehemiah, so that the king Artaxerxes granted his requests?

3. How did Nehemiah respond to the mockery and ridicule of his enemies?

4. What did the enemies conspire, what did they hear, and who did they see that frustrated their plot?

---


Nehemiah 5–8

---


1. What did Nehemiah hear the people crying about that made him very angry, and which command of Moses had the nobles and officials violated?

2. When did Nehemiah become governor of Judah, and did he eat the food allotted to the governor?

3. How long did it take to rebuild the city wall, and who, upon hearing, were afraid and lost their self-confidence, and why?

4. Whom did Nehemiah appoint to be in charge of Jerusalem, and why?

5. On the first day of the seventh month, to whom did Ezra the priest bring the Book of the Law?

6. When the returned exiles read from the Book of the Law, what festival did they learn they were to observe in the seventh month, and did they do as the LORD had commanded through Moses?

---


Nehemiah 9–13

---


1. What did the LORD find in Abraham that caused Him to make a covenant with him?

2. When the LORD came down on Mount Sinai, where did He speak to the Israelites from, and what did He give them?

3. Those who were able to understand bound themselves with a curse and an oath to follow what and to obey what?

4. Among the rest of the people, by casting lots, how many out of every ten were brought to live in Jerusalem, the holy city?

5. In Nehemiah chapter 13, what kinds of evils did Nehemiah correct among the Israelites?
